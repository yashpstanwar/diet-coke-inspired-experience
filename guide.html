Step 6 of 15

3D Diet Coke Can Procedural Geometry & Materials

Implement createCanMesh() in src/can.js: 1. Create THREE.MeshStandardMaterial instances: brushed metal (metalness: 0.68, roughness: 0.29), rim metal, and label body material with the procedural canvas texture. 2. Main body: THREE.CylinderGeometry(0.76, 0.76, 3.42, 128, 1, true). 3. Tapered neck & beveled chine: stack CylinderGeometry and TorusGeometry rings for the realistic double-rolled top rim and recessed bottom dome. 4. Mouth aperture: THREE.CircleGeometry(0.12, 32) placed at (0, 2.008, -0.25) with dark interior material, initially hidden. 5. Articulated pull-tab: THREE.Group with TorusGeometry pull ring and SphereGeometry rivet positioned at (0, 2.025, -0.06). 6. Return { can, tabPivot, aperture }.

Builds an authentic 3D soda can from mathematical primitives, complete with realistic rim contours and a mechanical pull-tab ready to animate.
